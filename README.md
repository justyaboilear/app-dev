# Fast & Furious Franchise

> *"I don't have friends. I got family."* — **Dominic Toretto**

The **Fast & Furious** franchise began in 2001 as a street-racing action film and evolved into a global blockbuster series known for high-speed heists, custom cars, and tight-knit crew loyalty.

---

## Core Themes & Highlights
* **Street Racing Roots:** Started with underground night races and tuner culture before scaling up to international covert operations.
* **Foundational Duo:** The dynamic between **Dominic Toretto** and **Brian O'Conner** anchors the emotional heart of the series.
* **Over-the-Top Action:** Famous for practical stunts, highway chases, and wild set pieces (*"Nothing makes sense anymore, and we love it"*).

---

## Iconic Rides
1. **1970 Dodge Charger R/T** — Dom's signature muscle car packed with raw power.
2. **1994 Toyota Supra Mk IV** — The iconic orange sports car rebuilt with Brian.
3. **1999 Nissan Skyline GT-R (R34)** — A fan-favorite tuner legend driven by Brian.
